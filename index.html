AboutEnglish

Yusuke Sunagawa（砂川祐介） is a Japanese full-stack engineer with about 20 years of experience, covering the full lifecycle from business requirements and architecture to implementation and operations. He is a Registered Information Security Specialist (RISS, Japan) and serves as a technology lead / external CTO for multiple companies.

In February 2026, he spoke at the Ghana Tree Crops Summit 2026 (TCDA international conference) in Accra, presenting a non-blockchain digital system to combat oil palm smuggling before Ghanaian government ministers and officials. The proposal was covered by major Ghanaian media including GhanaWeb and Adom Online.

From 2020 to 2025, he worked with Double Frontier Inc., a Japanese startup that had no in-house engineers when he joined. He single-handedly built an online grocery platform based on Saleor, the open-source e-commerce platform from Poland, later became CTO, built the engineering team from scratch, and led the business to an exit through an M&A by a logistics company in 2025.

He works in Japanese (native), English (business), and Chinese (conversational).
